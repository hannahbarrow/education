Data Story 2: 

Education Inequality: Does having more money mean that you are spending more money on education?
by Hannah Barrow
2026-03-03

This data story focuses on Sustainable Development Goals 4 (quality education) 
and 5 (gender equality) using data from 9 countries with varying global ranks in 
GDP, education spending, enrollment, and inequality. Through these countries, I 
explore the discrepancies in how much money a country has and how much of that 
money is used towards education the citizens.
